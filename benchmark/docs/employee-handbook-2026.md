# Kestrel Analytics – Employee Handbook 2026

## 1. Welcome to Kestrel Analytics

Welcome to Kestrel Analytics. As a growing force in data intelligence and predictive modeling, our success relies entirely on the talent, dedication, and collaborative spirit of our team members. Whether you are joining our headquarters, one of our international hubs, or working in a hybrid arrangement, you play a critical role in shaping how we deliver insights to our clients worldwide.

This Employee Handbook outlines the policies, benefits, expectations, and guidelines that govern our workplace. It is designed to provide clarity and transparency so that you can focus on doing your best work. Kestrel Analytics operates globally, with primary offices located in **Austin**, **Munich**, and **Pune**. While local statutory regulations may occasionally interact with these policies, the guidelines contained in this handbook apply across our corporate network unless explicitly noted otherwise.

Please read this document carefully, keep it as a reference for your tenure with the company, and reach out to the People Operations team if you have any questions.

---

## 2. Workplace Culture and Standards

Kestrel Analytics is built on a foundation of mutual respect, analytical rigor, and operational transparency. We expect all team members to uphold the highest standards of professional conduct, data privacy, and ethical behavior. 

We celebrate diverse perspectives and are committed to maintaining a workplace free from discrimination, harassment, and bias. Every employee is responsible for fostering an inclusive environment where ideas can be challenged constructively and colleagues are supported. Communication across all channels—whether Slack, email, or in-person meetings—should remain professional, respectful, and aligned with our core values.

---

## 3. Onboarding and Probationary Periods

Starting a new role involves significant learning and adaptation. To ensure a smooth transition, every new hire undergoes an initial evaluation period designed to help you integrate into your team, understand our technical stack, and meet baseline performance expectations.

The length of this initial evaluation varies by region due to local statutory frameworks:
* **Probation:** **90 days** in the US, **6 months** in Germany.

During this window, managers will provide regular feedback and check-ins. If performance or cultural alignment concerns arise, People Operations will work with the manager and employee to address them constructively.

---

## 4. Attendance, Hours, and Remote Work

We believe in outcome-oriented work environments that balance operational collaboration with individual flexibility. While core working hours vary by time zone to facilitate team alignment across our office locations, we maintain a flexible stance on daily scheduling.

For team members wishing to split their time between home and the office, Kestrel Analytics supports flexible arrangements subject to team needs and operational requirements:
* **Remote work:** up to 3 days per week remote; fully remote arrangements need VP approval; one-time home office stipend of **$600**.

If you opt for a standard hybrid schedule utilizing up to three remote days per week, the one-time home office stipend is available to assist with setting up an ergonomic and secure workspace at home. Employees seeking an entirely remote schedule must secure formal written approval from their respective Vice President before transitioning out of the office on a permanent basis.

---

## 5. Time Off and Leave Policies

Rest, rejuvenation, and personal time are vital for sustained creativity and analytical excellence. Kestrel Analytics provides comprehensive time-off structures tailored to our global footprint.

### 5.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
Annual leave allowances are structured regionally to reflect local standards and legal norms:
* **Paid time off:** United States employees get **20 days** per year; Germany **30 days**; India **24 days**.

### 5.2 Carry-Over and Expiration
We encourage employees to utilize their full PTO allotment within the calendar year. However, we recognize that planning challenges occur. Unused days may be rolled over subject to strict limits:
* **Carry-over:** up to **5 unused days** (US and India), up to **10 days** (Germany); carried days expire on **March 31**.

Any carried-over days not used by the March 31 expiration date will be forfeited and cannot be paid out or extended.

### 5.3 Parental Leave
Growing families require dedicated support. Kestrel Analytics offers robust parental leave provisions to ensure parents can bond with their new children without financial stress:
* **Parental leave:** **18 weeks** at full pay for the primary caregiver, **8 weeks** for the secondary caregiver.

This policy applies regardless of whether the addition to the family comes via birth, adoption, or foster placement. Employees must notify People Operations at least 30 days prior to the anticipated start of leave where feasible.

### 5.4 Sabbaticals
Long-term commitment to Kestrel Analytics is deeply valued. To reward sustained dedication, we offer extended time away for personal enrichment, travel, or rest after significant tenure:
* **Sabbatical:** **4 weeks paid** after **5 years** of service.

Sabbatical scheduling must be coordinated with your department head at least three months in advance to ensure seamless project handovers and continuity of client deliverables.

## 6. Performance Management and Growth

At Kestrel Analytics, professional growth is a continuous dialogue rather than an annual event. We utilize a structured framework to evaluate contributions, align goals, and plan career trajectories across our technical and operational teams.

### 6.1 Review Cycles
To ensure timely feedback and equitable recognition, formal evaluations occur bi-annually:
* **Performance reviews** happen twice a year, in **April** and **October**; calibration happens in **November**.

During the April and October review windows, employees complete self-assessments while managers compile performance observations, peer feedback, and project outcomes. Following these evaluations, leadership teams convene in November to calibrate ratings across departments, ensuring consistency, fairness, and objectivity in how we assess impact and allocate compensation adjustments or promotions.

### 6.2 Professional Development and Learning
Staying at the forefront of data analytics requires ongoing skill acquisition. We encourage all employees to pursue relevant certifications, courses, conferences, and technical workshops. To support this, the company provides a dedicated financial resource for continuous education:
* **Learning budget:** **$1,500** per employee per year.

Learning budget allocations must be approved in advance by your direct supervisor to ensure alignment with your individual development plan and team technical goals. Unused learning budget funds do not roll over from year to year.

## 7. Global Offices and Facilities

Kestrel Analytics maintains state-of-the-art office environments designed to facilitate deep focus, collaborative brainstorming, and secure data handling. Our primary workplace hubs are situated in three vibrant tech corridors:
* **Offices:** Austin, Munich, and Pune.

Each office is equipped with high-speed secure networks, dedicated quiet zones, collaborative meeting spaces, and standard amenities. Employees visiting or permanently based at any of these facilities are required to adhere to local physical security protocols, badge access policies, and visitor registration guidelines.

## 8. Information Security and Data Compliance

Given the nature of our work in data analytics, protecting sensitive client information, proprietary algorithms, and internal company data is paramount. Every employee is bound by strict confidentiality agreements and data security standards.

* **Credentials:** Never share your network login credentials, multi-factor authentication codes, or access badges.
* **Hardware:** Company-issued laptops must be used for all Kestrel Analytics work. Keep software updated and avoid installing unauthorized third-party applications.
* **Client Data:** Access client databases strictly on a need-to-know basis and in accordance with established data governance frameworks.

Failure to adhere to information security protocols compromises our business integrity and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.

## 9. Compensation, Expenses, and Travel

Kestrel Analytics is committed to fair, market-competitive compensation structures benchmarked against regional industry standards. 

### 9.1 Payroll and Expenses
Salaries are disbursed according to local payroll schedules in Austin, Munich, and Pune. For business-related expenses incurred during client visits, approved travel, or company-sponsored events, employees must submit itemized receipts through our corporate expense platform within 30 days of the expense being incurred. All travel and entertainment expenses must remain within reasonable pre-established budgetary thresholds and require managerial sign-off.
