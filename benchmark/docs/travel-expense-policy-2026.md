# Kestrel Analytics: Travel & Expense Policy 2026

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Kestrel Analytics is committed to supporting business travel that fosters client relationships, drives business growth, and advances our analytical mission. At the same time, we have a fiduciary responsibility to manage company funds prudently and transparently. This Travel & Expense (T&E) Policy 2026 applies to all full-time employees, part-time staff, contractors, and representatives of Kestrel Analytics who incur travel, entertainment, or operational expenses on behalf of the company.

Compliance with this policy is mandatory. Expenses incurred without adherence to these guidelines may not be reimbursed. Managers and approvers are expected to review all submissions rigorously to ensure they align with the parameters set forth in this document. Questions regarding specific situations should be directed to the Finance and Operations department prior to incurring the expense.

## 2. General Principles

When traveling or entertaining on behalf of Kestrel Analytics, employees act as ambassadors of the firm. All expenditures must be guided by good judgment, moderation, and fiscal responsibility. Employees should always seek the most cost-effective option that remains practical and safe for business operations.

Personal expenses will not be covered under any circumstances. Furthermore, splitting expenses to bypass approval thresholds or cost caps is strictly prohibited and constitutes a violation of company policy.

## 3. Air Travel Guidelines

Air travel represents a significant portion of our operational expenditures. To balance employee comfort with budgetary responsibility, specific rules govern flight bookings.

### Class of Service
All travelers must book economy class for flights under 6 hours. For flights exceeding 6 hours in total duration, alternative booking classes may be discussed with department leadership, provided the itinerary represents a prudent use of company resources. 

### Booking Procedures
Flights must be booked well in advance through the designated corporate travel agency or the company-approved booking portal to secure the most competitive rates. Non-refundable tickets should be selected whenever travel plans are stable and unlikely to change. 

## 4. Ground Transportation and Mileage

Getting to and from client sites, airports, and corporate offices should be accomplished efficiently using the most economical mode of transport available.

### Rental Cars and Taxis
Rental vehicles should be mid-size or compact unless traveling with a group that necessitates a larger vehicle. Public transit, rideshares, and taxis are encouraged for urban travel where parking and car rentals are cost-prohibitive.

### Personal Vehicle Usage
When employees use their personal vehicles for approved business travel, Kestrel Analytics provides reimbursement based on standard distance calculations. Mileage will be reimbursed at the rate of $0.67 per mile. This rate covers fuel, wear and tear, maintenance, and insurance costs. Commuting mileage from an employee’s primary residence to their designated permanent office location is never eligible for reimbursement.

## 5. Lodging and Accommodation

Selecting safe, comfortable, and reasonably priced lodging is essential for productive business travel. Employees should utilize preferred corporate hotel partners whenever possible.

### Hotel Rate Caps
To maintain cost control across different markets, maximum nightly rates have been established. The standard hotel cap is $250 per night. However, recognizing the higher cost of living and business operations in specific global hubs, exceptions apply to select metropolitan areas. The hotel cap is $320 per night in New York, London and Munich.

Employees are expected to book standard rooms. Upgrades to executive suites, club levels, or luxury accommodations are the personal financial responsibility of the traveler.

## 6. Meals and Per Diem

Meals during travel are managed through a per diem system designed to simplify reporting while covering reasonable sustenance costs based on the geographic location of the travel.

### Per Diem Rates
The standard meal per diem covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, and incidental expenses associated with daily dining while traveling on company business. The applicable rates are:
* Meal per diem: $70 domestic
* Meal per diem: $95 international

If a meal is provided as part of a conference, client event, or flight, the employee should not claim the per diem portion corresponding to that meal. Per diems are intended solely for the traveler and cannot be used to cover the meals of non-employees, unless part of an officially approved business entertainment event.

## 7. Client Entertainment and Alcohol

Building relationships with clients is vital to Kestrel Analytics, and occasional business meals or entertainment events are acceptable when they directly support strategic objectives.

### Alcohol Policy
Kestrel Analytics maintains a strict stance on the reimbursement of alcoholic beverages during travel and internal events. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except client dinners approved in advance. Even in cases where advance approval is granted for client dinners, alcohol consumption must remain moderate and professional.

## 8. Expense Approval Thresholds

Depending on the scale of the expenditure, additional layers of authorization are required before an expense can be processed for reimbursement. These thresholds ensure that significant financial commitments receive appropriate executive oversight.

### Approval Tiers
* Expenses over $750 need director approval.
* Expenses over $5,000 need CFO approval.

Employees must obtain these approvals in writing (email confirmation is acceptable) and attach the authorization documentation to their expense report. Incurring expenses above these thresholds without prior explicit approval will result in a delay or complete denial of reimbursement.

## 9. Receipt Management and Submission

Timely and accurate reporting ensures that financial records remain up-to-date and enables prompt reimbursement to employees.

### Submission Deadlines and Platforms
All expense reports, accompanied by fully itemized receipts, must be submitted within 30 days through the Ledgerly portal. 

### Receipt Requirements
Original, itemized receipts are required for all individual expenses. Credit card slips alone are generally insufficient; the receipt must show the specific items purchased, the date, the vendor name, and the total amount paid. If a receipt is lost, the employee must submit a formal missing receipt affidavit explaining the circumstances, which will require direct managerial sign-off.

## 10. Audit and Enforcement

The Finance department conducts regular audits of all submitted expense reports to ensure strict compliance with the T&E Policy 2026. 

### Non-Compliance
Submissions that violate these policies, lack necessary receipts, exceed caps without proper pre-authorization, or fail to meet the submission timeline will be rejected and returned to the employee for correction. Repeated or intentional violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including revocation of travel privileges, formal warnings, or termination of employment.

## 11. Policy Acknowledgment

By undertaking business travel or submitting expenses to Kestrel Analytics, employees acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agreed to abide by all stipulations contained within this Travel & Expense Policy 2026. 

For any inquiries or clarifications regarding these guidelines, please contact the Finance and Operations team via the internal help desk.
